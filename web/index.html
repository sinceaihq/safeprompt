SafePrompt

Check your AI prompt for personal data before you send it.

SafePrompt finds national IDs, bank accounts, cards, contact details and secret keys in text, lets you choose what to replace, and gives you a safe version to send. It runs on your device: no account, no server.

European identifiers, validated

National IDs of 22 countries, EU VAT numbers and IBANs are checked with their official check-digit rules, so order and invoice numbers are not flagged.

Stays on your device

Detection runs in the browser. The extension has no server, makes no network requests and never sends a message for you.

Placeholders you can undo

Values become <EMAIL_1>, <IBAN_1>… so the AI can still follow your text. Paste its answer back to restore the originals locally.

Honest about limits

Names and free-text details are often not detected, and no tool finds everything. SafePrompt helps you notice; it does not make anything anonymous or GDPR-compliant.
